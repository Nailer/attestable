One-take script

About 3 minutes. Record start to finish, no stopping. Grey boxes are what you say. Everything else is what you do.

Do this before you hit record

  • Open nailer.github.io/attestable. Wait for it to finish loading.
  • Connect MetaMask. Make sure it says Creditcoin CC3 Testnet.
  • Settings → Timestamps UTC, Auto-refresh Off. Then go back to Coverage.
  • Open a second tab: this Blockscout page. Leave it on Internal txns.
  • Come back to tab 1. Click cover #6 so it's already open.
  • Unlock MetaMask now so it doesn't ask for your password on camera.

Start recording with cover #6 on screen.

The take

0:00

Just talk. Don't move the mouse yet.

This is Attestable. It's insurance for the price feeds that apps depend on.

If you run a lending app, you need to know what ETH is worth. You get that from a price feed. If the feed goes quiet, your app can't work properly and you start losing money. Right now nobody pays you for that. You just take the loss.

Attestable lets someone else carry that risk, and you pay them for it.

0:30

Point at the cover on screen. Move slowly.

Here's a real one. Someone put up 200 CTC to back it. The buyer paid 12 CTC. The deal is simple — this feed must not go quiet for more than 90 minutes.

Now look at this number. Right now the contract has no proof the feed was alive at any point in this window. So it assumes the worst. It treats the whole 80 minutes as one long silence.

Rest the cursor on “80.0 min observed / 90.0 min allowed”.

0:55

Scroll down. Click Fetch and submit evidence. Approve in MetaMask.

So let me give it proof. This goes to Ethereum, finds a real price update inside that window, gets a proof of it, and sends that proof over to Creditcoin.

It will take about 30 seconds. Keep talking — don't sit in silence:

What's happening now is the part that matters. The proof shows this exact price update really was in that Ethereum block. Creditcoin checks that for itself. It doesn't trust me, and it doesn't trust some server I'm running. It checks the maths.

And I didn't put that price there. Chainlink publishes it for their own reasons. They don't know this project exists. That's exactly what makes it worth using as evidence.

1:30

Scroll back up. The number has changed. Point at it.

There it is. The worst gap just dropped from 80 minutes to 41, because now we can show the feed was alive in the middle of the window.

1:45

Click Settle this cover. Approve in MetaMask.

Now I settle it.

Another 30 seconds. Keep talking:

Nobody approves this. There's no claim form, no waiting period, and no person deciding whether I deserve it. The contract looks at the proof it has and pays whoever the terms say should be paid.

2:15

Badge flips to HEALTHY. Stop moving the mouse for a second.

Healthy. The gap stayed under 90 minutes, so the person who backed it keeps their collateral and keeps the premium. They got paid for taking a risk that didn't happen.

2:30

Switch to the Blockscout tab.

That one ended well. Here's one that didn't.

On the 31st of August this same feed went quiet for nearly 13 hours. That's real — I didn't invent it to make a demo.

This is that policy settling. 200 CTC went to the buyer. 12 CTC stayed with the person who backed it, because they still earned their premium. And this line at the top is the contract checking with Creditcoin that the outage could even be proven, before it agreed to pay anything.

Walk down the three rows as you say them: the ChainInfo call, 200 CTC, 12 CTC.

2:55

Stop. Don't click anything else.

That's it. A real feed, a real outage, real money moving, and no person in the middle. It's live right now and everything's linked below.

If something goes wrong mid-take

Don't stop recording. Say this and keep going:

That didn't go through — let me use one that's already closed.

Then click cover #4 and settle that instead. It's verified to work and also settles HEALTHY. It has no evidence, so add: “a 20-minute window can't contain a 90-minute silence, so the contract can prove this one is fine from the window alone.”

Two rules

  • Don't open cover #1. Its number is wrong — a bug in our own scanner that we found and fixed. Nothing on screen should show it.
  • Don't leave the Blockscout Details tab open. It shows Value: 0 CTC and makes it look like no money moved. The money is under Internal txns.
